About 4J Innovation Limited

Our story, our values, and the people leading Papua New Guinea's dependable partner for signage, printing, merchandise and technical solutions.

Who We Are

A single, dependable platform for every business need

4J Innovation Limited is a Papua New Guinea based retail, wholesale, merchandising, signage, fabrication, and technical solutions company committed to delivering reliable products and professional services across multiple industries.

Founded on a vision to provide quality solutions through innovation, efficiency, and integrity, we've built strong partnerships with clients through dependable service, technical expertise, and customer-focused operations.

We combine merchandising, signage fabrication, printing, networking solutions, consultancy and distribution support into one streamlined business platform — designed to support both private and commercial sectors nationwide.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality products and services through strong customer relationships, reliable supply-chain systems, innovative solutions, and professional business ethics — ensuring efficiency, value, and long-term client satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become one of Papua New Guinea's preferred providers of merchandising, signage, distribution, technical consultancy, and transportation services — delivering dependable nationwide solutions with professionalism and innovation.

Our Story

A Brief History

From registration to a growing portfolio of nationwide projects — here's how 4J Innovation came to be.

30 May 2024

Founded & Officially Registered

4J Innovation Limited is officially registered with the PNG Investment Promotion Authority (IPA Certificate No. 110964, Company No. 1-130338550), laying the legal and operational foundation for a multi-service company built on innovation, efficiency and integrity.

2024

Building Our Core Capabilities

We assembled a skilled team across five key divisions — merchandising, signage & fabrication, printing & branding, IT & technical solutions, and transportation & delivery — creating one streamlined platform for our clients.

2024 – Present

Earning Trust Through Delivery

From directional wayfinding signage at Pacific International Hospital's Medical Centre to large-scale pylon branding concepts for LANDCROS, we began building a portfolio of dependable, professionally executed projects for hospitals, corporates and private clients.

Looking Ahead

Growing Nationwide

Today, 4J Innovation continues to expand its reach across Port Moresby and beyond — partnering with hospitals, corporates and private clients who value reliability, technical expertise and consistent quality, with a vision to become a nationwide name in signage, distribution and technical services.

Meet Our Managing Director

Strategic leadership and hands-on operational oversight, driving 4J Innovation's growth across Papua New Guinea.

RJA
Managing Director

Ryan Jay Alinea

Founder & Managing Director, 4J Innovation Limited

Ryan Jay Alinea founded 4J Innovation Limited with a clear vision — to give Papua New Guinean businesses a single, dependable partner for signage, printing, merchandise and technical solutions. As Managing Director, Ryan leads the company's strategic direction and day-to-day operations, drawing on hands-on industry experience in fabrication, branding and client relationship management.

Under his leadership, 4J Innovation has grown from a registered start-up into a trusted service provider for hospitals, corporates and private clients across Port Moresby — with every project, from a single business card to a nationwide signage rollout, delivered with precision, integrity and care.
